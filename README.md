# Job Finder App — Product Research & Design

Design groundwork for a career-matching app that pairs a personality-aware
questionnaire with concrete career suggestions.

## What's here

- **[job_questions.md](job_questions.md)** — a question bank of 200+ assessment
  questions across hobbies & interests, talents & skills, and work
  preferences, designed to feed a matching engine.
- **[mbtianalysis.md](mbtianalysis.md)** — a structured knowledge base covering
  all 16 MBTI personality types: cognitive stacks, sub-personalities, shadow
  traits, strengths/weaknesses, ideal work environments, and suggested
  careers, with tags for programmatic matching.

## Status

Research and content-design phase. The next step is an app that scores
questionnaire answers against the personality knowledge base to produce
ranked career suggestions.
