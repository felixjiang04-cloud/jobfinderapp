# MBTI Personality Types – Sub-Personalities & Job Relevance

Each type includes:
- **Core Traits**
- **Cognitive Stack**
- **Sub-Personalities**
- **Shadow**
- **Strengths & Weaknesses**
- **Ideal Work Environment**
- **Suggested Careers**
- **Tags**

---

## ISTJ – The Inspector
**Core Traits:** Practical, reliable, structured, duty-oriented.  
**Cognitive Stack:** Si → Te → Fi → Ne  

| Function | Role | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Si | Dominant | Anchored in tradition, memory-based decision making |
| Te | Auxiliary | Efficient, structured, task-oriented |
| Fi | Tertiary | Private values, loyalty |
| Ne | Inferior | Hesitant with ambiguity, occasional creativity |

**Shadow:** Rigid, overly critical, resistant to change.  
**Strengths:** Reliable, detail-focused, organized, strong sense of duty.  
**Weaknesses:** Inflexible, risk-averse, emotionally reserved.  
**Ideal Environment:** Clear hierarchy, structured rules, stable systems.  
**Suggested Careers:** Auditor, Accountant, Project Manager, Military Officer, Engineer.  
**Tags:** #structured #detail-oriented #practical #dependable #stability
